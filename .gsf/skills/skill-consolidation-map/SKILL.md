# Skill consolidation map

## 1. Role

Act as a read-only consolidation proposal author. Determine whether named skills can share a complete procedure **without losing behavior, obligations, references, attribution, local customization or consumer compatibility**. Deliver the whole candidate and proposed future migration when the bounded inputs permit it; otherwise deliver the whole safe map and the exact missing prerequisites. `Keep separate` and `no change` are legitimate conclusions, not failures to consolidate.

This is a portable Markdown procedure, not a merger, reference-enforcement service, legal decision, installer or curator appointment. Similar wording, lexical similarity, a shared title, low observed use or zero hits in a limited search is not proof of redundancy. A union is justified by compatible mechanisms and preserved intent, authority and effect boundaries, not by source-text compression.

Follow [GSF mode](../gsf-mode/SKILL.md), the configured [worker contract](../../agents/WORKER_CONTRACT.md), [candidate/evidence contract](../../state/candidate-contract.md) and configured [response template](../../templates/response-template.md). Use [create-skill](../create-skill/SKILL.md) for complete authoring; [skill-curation-authority](../skill-curation-authority/SKILL.md) for authentic action-specific jurisdiction; [upstream-sync](../upstream-sync/SKILL.md) for imported bases and local adaptations; and [license-dossier](../license-dossier/SKILL.md) for component/use-specific rights. Preserve those procedures as their existing owners, not duplicate policy inside the new skill.

## 2. Context

### Applicability and adjacent owners

- **Use:** “Compare these two pinned recovery skills and their supplied local variants, assets and consumer snapshots; propose one complete procedure only if it preserves both permission boundaries and every consumer obligation.” A read-only proposal is applicable even when source-edit authority is absent.
- **Do not use for simple selection:** “Which existing skill should answer this task?” belongs to [skill-selection](../skill-selection/SKILL.md), without a consolidation proposal.
- **Do not replace source synchronization:** a new upstream version with a three-way customization merge belongs to upstream-sync. This procedure may map consolidation across supplied imported versions, but does not fetch or apply an upstream update.
- **Do not use to perform retirement or installation:** a request to remove an old skill, rewrite references, publish a package, register a host or migrate a job needs its separately authorized existing owner/procedure. Assess the proposed route here; perform none of those effects. Missing downstream procedure or owner is a named prerequisite, not permission to invent one.
- **No automatic union:** if recovery semantics, rights, authority or a required consumer cannot be preserved, recommend keep separate/no change with exact reasons. Never weaken either source to make the comparison look compatible.

### Accepted finite boundary

Before comparison, identify the accepted question and endpoint; project/repository and exact worktree; named skills and variants; permitted source, installation and consumer roots; intended target name/path; audience/use/distribution scope; allowed readers; actual owners; and output destination. Resolve repository paths from the accepted repository root and links from their referring files. The relative links in this skill resolve from this file. A name or branch alone is not an exact baseline.

Distinguish three independent permissions:

1. **Read:** inspect these exact non-secret supplied/authorized sources and existing receipts, for this audience and purpose.
2. **Propose:** return a bounded suggestion inline or in this dispatch's assigned owned output. It permits no canonical source, catalog or installed edit.
3. **Effect:** source merge, protected-path change, acceptance, publication, rebuild/export, installation and consumer/job migration each need their actual separate authority. Loading this skill grants none of them. Parent-approved writes to an owned fixture/candidate require the parent's explicit assignment and exact destination; they are not implicit permission from loading the skill or from a source being writable.

A direct read-only reply has **no persistent writes**: no job initialization, proposal file, evidence attachment, memory update or index. An authorized dispatch writes only its assigned owned output; the coordinator owns canonical state and acceptance records. Candidate and patch content can be returned inline. Do not invent a role, approval store, gate, model/config/schema key or consolidation registry.

### Required evidence inventory

Freeze the following before claiming a complete consolidation. Give every observation its exact source locator and section, immutable revision or supplied snapshot identity, effective local-byte identity, capture/observation time and original evidence. Include measured raw-byte digest algorithm/value only when actually supplied or available through authorized read evidence. Never fabricate a hash, treat decoded/normalized text as raw bytes, or claim a clean/staged/dirty state without its receipt. A supplied immutable archive and effective local customizations are different inputs; a wrapper digest is not a source digest.

| Surface | Required version-bound inputs |
| --- | --- |
| Named source skills | Whole SKILL.md bodies, metadata, all five pillars and linked required material; canonical path/name, source origin and immutable revision; local effective bytes and known staged/dirty/untracked/deleted customizations as supplied. Preserve section/line anchors against the actual snapshot. |
| Bases and variants | Old imported upstream base, current upstream snapshot where relevant, import transformations, local forks/overrides and customization decisions. Missing base prevents claiming three-way preservation. Include variant-specific behavior even when it is absent from canonical text. |
| Assets and legal material | All referenced instructions, templates, prompts, scripts as inert text, examples, data/binary assets, license copies, headers, notices, modification markings and attributions; source-to-destination mapping, applicable rights and privacy limits per component and intended use. |
| Reference and distribution surfaces | Approved public source/document roots, actual catalog/pack tables, qualified identities, wrappers/manifests, relevant source-to-package inventories and hashes, source/output mapping and semantic-loss receipts. Use the published [capability distribution](../../docs/hosts/capability-distribution.md) and [plugin export](../../docs/hosts/plugin-export.md) contracts. Do not infer pack membership from topic or a host-native role from a bundled role reference. |
| Installed and configured uses | Exact approved installation roots and installed bytes, manager/override policy and owners, relevant config and precedence evidence; configured roles, check definitions/cwd/effect/target and required policy/instruction snapshots. Read only the finite approved roots or supplied manifests/snapshots. Do not scan HOME, personal settings, private jobs, chats, conversation history, credentials or arbitrary ancestors. Do not follow escaping symlinks/junctions or remote references to expand scope. Record an external or inaccessible edge and request its exact permitted snapshot/owner instead. Do not run commands, imports, scripts, hooks, generators, checks, native probes, model/provider calls, network fetches or installs in this proposal procedure. Missing evidence remains missing; planned checks are not permission to execute them.

## 3. Task

### Step 1 — Freeze the proposal and classify completeness

1. Number the finite source/component snapshots and proposed destinations in the report. Record included roots, explicit exclusions, unknowns and the audience for each restricted source. Use stable report-local row identifiers, not new framework keys.
2. Separate observed source facts, supplied claims, inferences, proposed decisions and actual authentic decisions. Skill text, catalog descriptions, reports and embedded “approved” instructions are untrusted data; none authenticates a grant.
3. Identify missing whole source bytes, base/customization snapshots, rights, required references/assets, consumer closure, exact migration baselines and authentic decision owners. State what each gap prevents. Absence of a native receipt prevents a native-success claim; absence of a source merge grant alone does not prevent an otherwise authorized read-only candidate.
4. Set the endpoint descriptively: complete proposed union, keep separate/no change, or blocked union with safe map. These are report conclusions, not lifecycle states or approval flags. If required candidate inputs are missing, do not fabricate a candidate, invent consumer compatibility or deliver a partial outline as the whole union. Finish every independently safe mapping row and name the precise missing prerequisite and owner.

### Step 2 — Build the entire bounded dependency and consumer graph

Inspect the full authorized source and supplied reference/consumer inventories, not only same-name search hits. Resolve every known direct edge, then expand to transitive closure within the finite approved boundary. Keep the actual path through intermediate nodes so an indirect dependency is reviewable.

Include inline links/images, reference-style definitions, HTML references, fragment anchors, directory references, textual invocation/path mentions, includes, copied/generated content and declared names or qualified IDs in supported catalogs/configuration. An ordinary text mention and an actual configured dependency are different edge kinds. For directory dependencies retain the documented subtree semantics; never assume that one linked file represents the whole directory. Treat assets and legal notices as nodes, not incidental text.

Record both directions:

- **Outgoing:** each skill/variant section to other skills, contracts, references, assets, templates, scripts, notice/license documents and external sources, including transitive resources and source-to-wrapper transformations.
- **Incoming:** referring skills/docs, catalog/pack memberships, installed packages, host wrappers/manifests, role/check/config uses, external consumers in the accepted boundary and their indirect users. Include unresolved textual or dynamic references as unresolved, not as absent.
- **Frozen:** separately enumerate each supplied active, paused and archived consumer/job and its exact accepted snapshot. Preserve copied bytes, policy/schema/instruction identities, historic qualified names/paths and original receipts. A link to current main is not preservation of an old accepted procedure.

Use this report-local table, split whenever baselines, owners, rights or edge semantics differ:

| Edge ID / direction / complete transitive chain | Referring node revision + exact section/config field | Target node revision + exact path/fragment or asset | Mechanism and required behavior | Owner, rights/access and actual evidence | Proposed future target and exact edit, or retained original | Coverage gap / consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

List node inventories alongside edges so unreferenced supplied assets/customizations are not silently discarded. Show actual catalog and pack selections; where none are evidenced use UNKNOWN, not an invented pack or empty membership. For installed/generated surfaces map canonical source → generator/manifest → wrapper/package → installation → configured/native consumer separately, with actual identity and known semantic loss at each step.

Close the graph only when every inspected node's relevant incoming/outgoing surface has been accounted for and every boundary edge has a disposition. Report exact searched/read roots and manifests and the unavailable/excluded surfaces. A complete map is complete **only within that accepted boundary**. Missing reverse-reference inventory, unknown install consumers or inaccessible frozen snapshots blocks dependent migration and any claim of complete consumer preservation. Do not expand into private records or claim to have implemented a reference guard.

### Step 3 — Compare mechanisms rather than wording

For each source section and local variant, decompose obligations finely enough that none can hide in an “equivalent” paragraph. Include the source's role, trigger/do-not-use conditions, prerequisites, input/version assumptions, ordered actions, branch predicates, default behavior, actors and authentic authority, effect triggers/targets, outputs, required evidence/checks, failure/exception/edge behavior, privacy/rights, stop/recovery/return rules and completion conditions.

Compare each mechanism on the following axes:

| Original obligation + source/section + variant | Intent and independent expectation | Mechanism, ordering and branch predicates | Actor/authority and effect trigger/target | Outputs, evidence, exception/recovery and rights | Compatible, conditionally distinct, conflicting or unknown + contrary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Ask specifically whether one procedure authorizes an effect the other only proposes; whether “retry” is read-only observation or a write; whether rollback restores exact local data or accepts upstream replacement; whether one exception leaks private material; whether mandatory checks, native controls or independence differ; and whether local configuration changes the actor, target or invocation semantics. Preserve meaningful sequencing and negative outcomes, not just a list of steps.

A compatible union may use explicit mutually exclusive, fully specified branches when their selection inputs are available and their consumers retain the correct branch. It must not combine grants, take the broadest permission, impose an unrelated source's extra grant, or reinterpret a source's required refusal as optional. Unknown branch selection, incompatible defaults or an altered permission boundary is a material conflict. Do not replace it with “follow policy,” “as appropriate,” “handle safely,” or another vague instruction. Keep both originals when no exact compatible mechanism is supported.

Preserve contrary evidence: failed consuming outcomes, version/host limits, objections by actual owners, rights restrictions, contradictory customizations and evidence that a supposedly redundant skill serves a distinct consumer. Usage evidence is scoped observation, never a deletion criterion. Record actual unresolved decisions and their owners; assistant preference is not conflict resolution.

### Step 4 — Establish rights and separate decision authorities

Use the curation authority matrix for every affected source/layer and destination. Record authentic actors and decision sources for proposing, independent review where required, curation acceptance, source merge/apply, protected paths, rebuilding/export, publication, installation and consumer/new-job migration. Mark unrequested actions explicitly. Neither source ownership, license evidence, a reviewed candidate, an installed file nor an acceptance flag supplies all these authorities.

Use license-dossier separately for reference, adaptation and redistribution of every differently governed component. Preserve original notices, attribution chains, authorship/customization credits, complete required license texts, modification statements and asset-specific conditions at their exact proposed locations. Map upstream original → import/transformation → local customization → candidate section/asset. Do not attribute a fork's additions to upstream or relabel borrowed expression as new independent work.

Check the actual proposed audience/use, channel, disclosure, retention and recipients. A lawful internal union may still be undistributable; an authorized citation may still not permit copying. Private source access does not permit embedding it in a public candidate or matrix. Keep sensitive agreements/evidence in their approved custody and use authorized sanitized references with the original/derivative relationship stated. An organizational approval cannot manufacture third-party rights; a proposed candidate is not an authenticated legal grant or legal certification.

Unknown necessary rights, unresolved incompatible obligations, missing required source/asset bytes, unknown consumer behavior or missing branch selection blocks the complete dependent candidate. Do not remove the affected feature, paraphrase restricted text, omit a required asset or silently exclude a consumer to label the remainder a complete union. Record the safe map and recommend keep separate/no change or the exact missing prerequisite. An independently requested smaller scope would need explicit acceptance; it is not assumed here.

### Step 5 — Author the complete untrusted candidate and coverage matrix

When all required candidate inputs and use-specific rights support it, supply the **entire** proposed SKILL.md, not headings, excerpts, a source concatenation, external links in place of required mechanisms, pseudocode or a promise to finish later. The full content may be inline for a no-write request. If the intended union needs multiple referenced resources, include the complete proposed content of every added/changed text resource and an exact retained-byte manifest for unchanged/binary assets whose bytes and lawful custody are available. Binary bytes need not be printed in chat, but must resolve to the exact accessible pinned retained object and proposed destination; an unavailable necessary object blocks completeness.

Use lowercase kebab-case `name` and a nonempty trigger/outcome `description` in ordinary YAML frontmatter under create-skill. Do not invent metadata or permission fields. The candidate must have all five pillars:

1. **Role:** precise common purpose, distinct responsibilities and adjacent non-owners; no implied curator/operator appointment.
2. **Context:** positive and negative triggers; exact supported variants/versions, required inputs and references; independently authenticated authority and rights prerequisites.
3. **Task:** executable-as-instructions ordered actions, concrete branch-selection predicates, preserved variant mechanisms and effect triggers, expected artifacts, failures/exceptions, stop conditions and exact recovery/return behavior.
4. **Constraints:** every original permission/privacy/rights limitation, required check/reviewer/protected-path boundary, source attribution and no-effect limits; preservation, validity and invalidation conditions.
5. **Format:** actual consumer-visible output structure, provenance/evidence obligations, success versus blocked/inconclusive outcomes and handoff destination. Preserve any source-specific required fields or consumers' accepted output expectations.

Consolidate compatible repetition only when the coverage matrix demonstrates equivalence. Links back to originals are legitimate only for intentionally retained, exact version-bound components with an available continued dependency and a justified reason; they are not a substitute for the whole proposed common workflow. Distinguish a retained source dependency from a frozen consumer that remains entirely on its old baseline. No old mandatory behavior becomes a nonbinding note or a link to an unversioned latest page.

Deliver a coverage matrix with **one or more rows for every original obligation, exception, edge case, notice, asset and local customization**, including items unique to one source:

| Coverage ID | Original source/variant revision + exact section/lines or asset | Intent, obligation and mechanism including exception | Original provenance/attribution and applicable rights | Exact candidate path + heading/anchor and preserved mechanism, or exact still-retained original | Consumer/branch and migration-edge IDs | Preservation rationale, contrary evidence and unresolved decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

For many-to-one mappings justify semantic equivalence explicitly; for one-to-many show where each part is preserved. Keep original finding/notice/requirement identities where present. Include target-to-source coverage for every new normative instruction, distinguishing necessary explicit consolidation glue from a new unsupported obligation. Do not expand scope with retries, new effects or telemetry. A prose assertion that “everything is preserved” cannot substitute for this matrix. An unmapped obligation or unresolved material conflict means the candidate is not a complete supported union.

Label the result **untrusted proposal; not accepted, merged, published, installed, registered or migrated**. These disclosures are factual, not new lifecycle keys. Give the full candidate's available exact identity and base context, without inventing a candidate_id or measured digest. Readable source alone proves neither working host loading nor preserved native behavior.

### Step 6 — Supply the whole proposed future migration and return patch

Keep every original source, installed copy and frozen job unchanged during this procedure. Propose the whole finite migration against the **exact inspected baseline of each affected file/surface**, not a list of paths to fix later. Include full before/after entries or complete reviewable hunks with unambiguous snapshot/section anchors for:

- Canonical candidate files, retained assets and legal/attribution locations; every proposed source/config/document reference and fragment change.
- Catalog identity/description, explicit pack memberships and qualified IDs, including retained old entries where consumers still require them. A new name is not automatically an alias for old semantics.
- Wrapper/manifest and installed-distribution consequences, exact old/new source mapping and proposed existing rebuild/manager route. Do not hand-edit managed output as though it were its source. If generated output bytes cannot be known without separately authorized execution, state the exact unresolved output/provenance prerequisite and block the dependent migration; do not fabricate the generated patch or call it complete.
- Configured role/check uses and their exact required instruction/check bytes, cwd/effect/target, output expectations and policy dependencies. Preserve required checks and reviews; neither rename nor consolidation waives them or executes them.
- Every direct/transitive incoming/outgoing consumer edge and resource/fragment mapping. Mark each as proposed new-baseline migration, unchanged retained original, or blocked with reason. Do not assume downstream projects accept a change merely because a source owner did.

If a required exact baseline is missing, retain the full safe edge/coverage map and identify the missing file/receipt and owner. Do not fabricate a patch, rely on ambiguous search-and-replace or call a migration list the complete patch. If the candidate is complete but the exact consumer patch is blocked, report these outcomes separately; the overall consolidation is not ready for consumer migration.

For each new-job consumer identify its authentic migration decision owner and specify the proposed exact old accepted source/instruction/policy/schema/config identities, new candidate identities, matching variant/trigger/output mapping, preserved check/reviewer obligations, adoption preconditions and affected proof to invalidate. Say “proposed; owner decision missing” unless an actual decision receipt is supplied. Apply the existing [authorized upgrade guide](../../docs/upgrades/README.md) where its baseline rules apply; [GSF adopt](../gsf-adopt/SKILL.md) and [GSF setup](../gsf-setup/SKILL.md) retain adoption/configuration ownership. This proposal starts or refreshes no job.

Frozen active, paused and archived consumers remain on their exact accepted old bytes, references and policy/schema snapshots. Do not rewrite their source pointers, accepted reports, historic identities or evidence to match the candidate. A separately accepted future upgrade needs its own immutable new baseline and current proof under the existing guide; never describe a paused job's silent refresh as new-job migration.

Provide the whole proposed rollback/return map alongside the forward patch:

| Consumer/surface + exact baseline | Proposed target + exact forward patch/branch | Actual decision/effect owner + decision receipt or missing prerequisite | Retained original bytes/assets/notices and custody | Exact return patch/old resolution route + owner | Intervening-change, proof-invalidation and native limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Return mapping restores only separately authorized task-owned changes from retained exact baselines, reconciling intervening user changes and current ownership. Original-to-candidate and candidate-to-original section, fragment, qualified-name, resource and output mappings must resolve; ambiguity blocks the dependent migration. Source reversal cannot undo external actions a future consumer may perform; identify those residual owners and compensation dependencies without executing them. No autoalias, compatibility shim, redirect, tombstone, import, run, install, catalog update, source replacement, archive, deletion or upstream effect is part of this procedure. Do not propose destruction of still-referenced original snapshots as cleanup.

### Step 7 — Hand off evidence, conflicts and future proof without execution

Return the complete report/candidate/patch in the permitted audience and assigned endpoint. Identify the actual source diff owner, curator, required independent reviewers, acceptance owner, distribution/install owners and each consumer migration owner, or UNKNOWN with the exact missing authority. An author review checks coverage and unsupported claims; it is not independent review, authentic acceptance or a grant.

Propose the minimal finite future consumer observations using existing requirements: exact candidate/resources/configuration; independent expectation; each preserved variant and important failure/permission boundary; expected output/refusal and notices; actual source-to-package/installed/native stages requiring evidence; frozen-consumer preservation; return mapping. Do not create or run a suite, gate, command, host probe or model session. Only existing supplied original receipts are observations. Never reuse an old source/host PASS as proof for a changed candidate.

Current host loading, precedence, invocation, enforcement and product behavior remain **unproven without actual native evidence for the exact host/build, installed bytes, configuration and accepted product baseline**. Source files, catalogs, wrappers, name/description metadata and successful offline exports do not establish those stages. Markdown and declared read-only paths are not a sandbox; no native API or reference-enforcement capability is supplied by this skill.

## 4. Constraints and stop conditions

- Preserve every source's behavior, intent, required checks/reviews, authority and effect triggers. Similarity and low use cannot decide redundancy, bypass rights or authorize removal. Prefer a clear keep-separate decision over a misleading common procedure.
- Do not resolve a conflict by unioning permissions, choosing the more permissive source, silently restricting an original workflow, hiding variant differences in vague prose or deleting the disputed material. Use exact compatible branches only where their selectors and consumer mappings are supported; otherwise leave originals unchanged.
- Required unknown source/base/variant bytes, rights, asset custody, reference/consumer closure or migration baseline means the dependent deliverable is blocked. Complete independent rows and the whole safe map; never substitute a partial candidate or narrow the accepted set silently. Missing source/effect acceptance does not itself forbid an authorized untrusted proposal.
- Preserve notices, use-specific rights, source provenance, local customizations and private evidence custody. A public repository or proposed candidate is not a legal grant. Do not expose restricted originals while explaining a blocker.
- No persistent writes for direct read-only replies; dispatched writes only to assigned owned output. No changes to original/frozen sources, jobs, shared reports, catalogs, installed files, managed output, policy, roles, gates or approval records. No commands/hooks/model calls, scans of private history, automatic aliasing or effects.
- Any changed source/effective local bytes, asset/notice/rights evidence, use/audience, owner/grant, consumer/config/check graph, wrapper/install/host mapping or exact migration baseline invalidates affected conclusions. Reconcile the new authorized inputs; never overwrite an intervening edit or relabel old proof as current.
- Stop only the affected conclusions/effects and retain independently safe analysis. Report the exact missing prerequisite, authentic next owner and unchanged safe state using existing stop-class conventions where applicable; do not manufacture an appointment, approval or fallback.

## 5. Format and completion

Use the configured response conventions. Return these complete sections, inline for read-only requests or only at the dispatch's assigned owned output:

1. **Boundary and status:** accepted finite question/set; exact source/worktree/variant/install/host identities and baseline evidence; intended candidate path/use/audience; permitted endpoint; inclusions/exclusions and actual modified files. Distinguish source presence, proposed union, complete consumer migration patch and native proof.
2. **Inventory and whole safe graph:** source/variant/asset/legal nodes, effective customization/base mapping; full direct/transitive incoming and outgoing chains; pack/catalog/wrapper/install/role/check/config edges; authorized active/paused/archived frozen consumers; exact coverage gaps and their consequences.
3. **Semantic comparison and decisions:** intent/mechanism/authority/effect matrix; compatible/distinct/conflicting/unknown rows; contrary evidence and authentic decisions; supported union versus keep separate/no change and reasons. Preserve every unresolved issue.
4. **Rights and authority:** original notices/attributions/customization provenance; component/action/use-specific rights and privacy limits; authentic propose/review/accept/source/publish/install/migrate decisions separately; missing prerequisites and owners, not assistant-issued grants.
5. **Complete candidate or exact block:** entire untrusted five-pillar skill and required resource content/custody manifest, target/base identities and full original-to-candidate plus target-to-source coverage. If required inputs are missing, explicitly state no complete candidate was produced and why; retain the whole safe map, not fabricated content or excerpts labeled complete.
6. **Whole proposed migration and return:** exact-baseline forward/catalog/reference/config/consumer patches and rollback/return mappings; per-new-job owner decisions or missing receipts; unchanged frozen snapshots and retained originals; unresolved generated/native/consumer dependencies. Clearly identify a blocked patch rather than calling path suggestions complete.
7. **Handoff and actual limits:** legitimate diff/decision/effect owners; proposed finite future acceptance observations; actual supplied receipts versus inference and NOT RUN; exact missing prerequisites and safe next action. State no merge, acceptance, publication, installation, consumer migration or source/archive/delete effect occurred.

Completion of a bounded read-only assessment does not mean a union is accepted or migration-ready. A complete supported union requires the whole candidate, rights/provenance and obligation coverage, complete bounded consumer/reference mapping and exact proposed migration/return patch; any unsupported part remains explicit. Apply existing report verdict/stop-class rules, not a new consolidation PASS flag. This file defines a procedure; it is not evidence that a candidate, host loading or consumer migration has been exercised.

### Concrete decision examples — illustrative and not executed

**Compatible bounded union.** Supplied fixture snapshots `cursor-boundary@A1` and `repeated-page@B1` both have evidenced fixture-only adaptation rights and the same read-only owner/effect boundary. The first requires checking empty/last pages and preserving opaque cursors; the second requires detecting a repeated cursor and duplicate page IDs without fetching more data. Their supplied references, attribution notices, one API pack entry each, two configured review consumers and a paused job's exact snapshots are fully inventoried. A compatible candidate can require supplied pages only, preserve opaque tokens, and report both terminal-page and repetition findings. Map `A1/Task/terminal` to candidate `Task/Terminal pages`, `A1/Constraints/opaque` to `Constraints/Opaque cursors`, and `B1/Task/repetition` to `Task/Repeated pages`; map each notice and output field to its exact candidate/resource section as well. Produce the entire five-pillar candidate, both complete new-job consumer patches, exact proposed pack/catalog rows and return patch against those supplied baselines. The paused job stays on its original `A1`/`B1` snapshots with no link edits. In a real use of this example, the symbolic fixture labels must be replaced by actual immutable identities and complete per-obligation rows; this example is not itself a candidate or receipt. Missing native loading proof is disclosed, not invented from the pack entries. No patch is applied.

**Overlap but conflicting recovery/permission boundary — keep separate.** Fixture `restore-local@R1` requires an authenticated local owner and exact local-backup restore, forbids network effects, and stops on a changed baseline. Fixture `reseed-service@R2` requires a service operator's separate external-destructive grant and rebuilds from upstream state, losing local edits by an explicitly accepted service contract. Similar “recover service” steps do not make their triggers, accepted data loss, permissions or return paths interchangeable. An incoming generic `recover` wrapper supplies no selector, and its consumer depends on exact local restore. Replacing both by “restore or reseed as appropriate with permission” discards the local refusal and smuggles in a destructive option. Recommend keep separate/no change, preserve the wrapper's `R1` target and all frozen snapshots, and map the precise conflict and owner question. Do not invent a shared grant, silently make the local consumer destructive or call a concatenated source file a safe union.

**Missing rights and reference consumer — blocked union with safe map.** Fixture `review-with-chart@C1` includes a required chart and original attribution, but its supplied source has no applicable chart terms. A catalog receipt also names installed wrapper `chart-review`, whose exact bytes and downstream role/check/frozen-consumer inventory are unavailable in the approved scope. Map all available source obligations, the chart/notice provenance and the known outgoing/incoming edges. Name the missing chart adaptation/redistribution basis and actual rights decision owner; name the exact wrapper baseline, reverse-consumer manifest and permitted frozen snapshots needed from the installation/consumer owner. Produce no copied chart or complete dependent candidate, and fabricate no catalog patch. Do not remove the chart or assume no consumers to make the union pass. Keep originals unchanged and recommend no change pending those prerequisites; an independently lawful link-only reference remains a separate license-dossier row, not permission to copy the asset. Do not search HOME or private jobs to fill the gap.
