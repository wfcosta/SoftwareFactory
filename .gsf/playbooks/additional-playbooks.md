# Playbook: Hillclimb & Feature & Autonomous

- **Hillclimb:** Sustained, scientific improvement of one metric against a target, looping hypotheses with before/after measurement and one commit per accepted win.
- **Feature:** New or changed behavior, built from a named data shape and verified end-to-end.
- **Prototype:** A throwaway sketch to make a design or behavioral decision cheaply, or to settle an empirical fork.
- **Autonomous Run:** Drive a long task to completion without stopping, validating at each phase boundary.
- **Shipping:** Independently verify a green stack, then land the contiguous verified run bottom-up.
- **Opening a PR:** Open a ready PR from small ordered commits with conventional commits title and briefing-style body.
